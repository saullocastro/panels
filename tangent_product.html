Product of the tangent stiffness matrix by a vector#

Shell.calc_kTv() and MultiDomain.calc_kTv() return the product \([K_T(c)] \{v\}\) of the tangent stiffness matrix of Shell.calc_kT() by a vector, without assembling \([K_T]\), for all the geometrically non-linear models. Its cost is that of one evaluation of the internal force vector, instead of the assembly of \([K_T]\), whose cost grows with the square of the number of terms of the approximation. It is the exact product, to round-off, and the exact directional derivative of Shell.calc_fint().

The product is what the prescribed displacements imposed exactly need at each configuration, \(\partial R_r/\partial \lambda = [T]^T (\{F_{ext}\} - [K_T(c)] \{c_{p1}\})\), see Prescribed displacements imposed exactly, and what matrix-free iterative solvers and directional checks of the tangent need.

Derivation#

All the geometrically non-linear models, the plates and the Donnell and Sanders cylinders of the CLPT, FSDT and TSDT, have at an integration point the generalized strains

\[\{\varepsilon(c)\} = [B(\beta/2)] \{c\}, \qquad \{\beta\} = [G] \{c\}\]

where \(\{\beta\}\) are the two rotations of the von Kármán terms, \(w_{,x}\) and \(w_{,y}\) for the Donnell kinematics, \(w_{,y} - v/r\) replacing \(w_{,y}\) for Sanders, and \([B(\beta)]\) is the strain operator, linear in \(\{\beta\}\), which enters only its membrane rows:

\[\begin{split}\frac{\partial [B]}{\partial \beta_x} = \begin{bmatrix} [G_x] \\ 0 \\ [G_y] \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \frac{\partial [B]}{\partial \beta_y} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ [G_y] \\ [G_x] \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Half the rotations in \([B]\) give the quadratic terms \(\beta_x^2/2\), \(\beta_y^2/2\) and \(\beta_x \beta_y\). The internal force vector is \(\{F_{int}\} = \int [B(\beta)]^T [F] \{\varepsilon(c)\} dA\), and its directional derivative along \(\{v\}\) is

(1)#\[[K_T] \{v\} = \frac{d}{dt} \{F_{int}(c + t v)\} \Big|_{t=0} = \int \left( [B(\beta)]^T [F] [B(\beta)] \{v\} + [G]^T [N] [G] \{v\} \right) dA\]

because the strains are a quadratic form whose bilinear form is \([B(\beta)]\), such that \(\frac{d}{dt} \{\varepsilon(c + t v)\} = [B(\beta)] \{v\}\), and \(\frac{d}{dt} [B]^T \{\sigma\} = [G]^T [N] [G] \{v\}\) from the derivatives of \([B]\) above, with \([N]\) the membrane stresses. The two terms are those assembled by fkC_num and fkG_num of each model. The constant membrane stresses Nxx_cte, Nyy_cte and Nxy_cte, whose geometric stiffness Shell.calc_kC() adds to the tangent stiffness matrix, are added to \([N]\).

At each integration point, with the approximation functions tabulated once, the kernels calc_kTv of panels/models/<model>_num.pyx compute

  1. \(\{\beta\} = [G] \{c\}\) and \([G] \{v\}\);

  2. \(\{\varepsilon\} = [B(\beta/2)] \{c\}\) and \(\{\delta \varepsilon\} = [B(\beta)] \{v\}\);

  3. the stresses \([F] \{\varepsilon\}\), of which \([N]\), and \([F] \{\delta \varepsilon\}\);

  4. the sum of (1) over the terms.

Each step is a loop over the \(m n\) terms, against a loop over the \(m^2 n^2\) pairs of terms for the assembly of \([K_T]\).

The expressions are generated by theory/shells/tangent_product, which also verifies with SymPy, for every model, that (1) is the derivative of the internal force vector of one term, with a symbolic constitutive matrix; \([B]\) and \([G]\) being linear in the degrees of freedom, the identity extends to any number of terms.

Follower pressure loads#

With follower pressure loads, Shell.calc_kTv() adds the product of their load stiffness Shell.calc_kCfollower() as the central difference

\[-\frac{\partial \{F_p\}}{\partial \{c\}} \{v\} = -\frac{\{F_p(c + t v)\} - \{F_p(c - t v)\}}{2 t}\]

which is exact for any \(t\), the force vector \(\{F_p(c)\}\) being affine in \(\{c\}\) for follower='linear' and quadratic for follower='quadratic', see Follower pressure loads: theory. \(t = \max(\|c\|/\|v\|, 1)\) avoids the cancellation of large and small terms.

Verification#

The checks of the tangent stiffness matrix of tests/tests_shell/test_tangent_consistency.py are repeated for the product, for all 9 models, in tests/tests_shell/test_tangent_product.py:

  • the product equals calc_kT(c) @ v with a relative difference below \(10^{-12}\), also with a constant membrane stress state, one constitutive matrix per integration point, a partial integration domain, an offset in a larger vector, and dead and follower pressure loads at a load factor inc;

  • the directional Taylor test of Shell.calc_fint(), whose remainder falls as \(O(h^2)\);

  • the matrix built column by column from the product against the central difference of Shell.calc_fint();

  • in the undeformed state the product is \([K_0] \{v\}\);

  • Newton-Raphson with the tangent built from the product converges quadratically:

@pytest.mark.parametrize('model', MODELS)
def test_newton_raphson_converges_quadratically(model):
    """Newton-Raphson around a known equilibrium, with the tangent built from
    the product, must square the error; see the test of the same name in
    ``test_tangent_consistency.py``"""
    s, fint, kTv, KT = make_callables(model)

    def KT_from_product(c):
        N = c.shape[0]
        K = np.empty((N, N))
        for j in range(N):
            e = np.zeros(N)
            e[j] = 1.
            K[:, j] = kTv(c, e)
        return K

    for seed in range(13, 33):
        rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
        c_eq = random_state(s, rng)
        used = ~np.isclose(KT(c_eq).diagonal(), 0)
        c_eq[~used] = 0
        K0 = s.calc_kC(c=np.zeros_like(c_eq)).toarray()[np.ix_(used, used)]
        lam = eigh(KT(c_eq)[np.ix_(used, used)], K0, eigvals_only=True)
        if np.abs(lam).min() > 0.05:
            break
    else:
        raise AssertionError('no regular equilibrium state found')
    fext = fint(c_eq)

    c = c_eq + 1.e-2*random_state(s, rng)
    c[~used] = 0
    errors = [np.linalg.norm(c - c_eq)/np.linalg.norm(c_eq)]
    while errors[-1] > 1.e-8 and len(errors) < 12:
        K = KT_from_product(c)[np.ix_(used, used)]
        R = fint(c) - fext
        c[used] -= np.linalg.solve(K, R[used])
        errors.append(np.linalg.norm(c - c_eq)/np.linalg.norm(c_eq))

    assert len(errors) - 1 <= 6, 'too many iterations: %s' % errors
    floor = 1.e-9 if modelDB.db[model]['dofs'] == 5 else 1.e-12
    orders = [np.log(e2/e1)/np.log(e1/e0)
              for e0, e1, e2 in zip(errors[:-2], errors[1:-1], errors[2:])
              if e2 > floor]
    assert len(orders) >= 1, 'no step above the round-off floor: %s' % errors
    assert min(orders) > 1.5, 'convergence is not quadratic: %s' % errors

tests/multidomain/test_tangent_product_multidomain.py checks MultiDomain.calc_kTv() with penalty connections and follower loads, and \(\partial R_r/\partial \lambda\) of the prescribed displacements against the assembled tangent stiffness matrix.
