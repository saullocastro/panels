Large rotations: total-Lagrangian Kirchhoff-Love plates#

The model 'plate_clpt_tl' is the plate of 'plate_clpt_gl', with the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains, whose changes of curvature are measured with the normal of the deformed mid-surface instead of the fixed local frame. All its strains are then invariant under any rigid rotation of any deformed state: a bending deformation carried through a large rotation, e.g. a local buckle of a skin of a wing box bent by a large rotation, keeps its bending energy, which 'plate_clpt_gl' multiplies by \(\cos^2\theta\). The kernels panels.models.plate_clpt_tl_num are generated from a symbolic derivation with SymPy of the strains below, with the helpers of the generator of 'plate_clpt_gl'.

Note

Integration points: exact by default, a faster option. By default the integrals of 'plate_clpt_tl' are exact, with \(3p\) Gauss-Legendre points along each direction, 21 for m = 8, against the \(2p + 1\), 15, of 'plate_clpt_gl', see Internal forces and tangent stiffness. A tangent stiffness matrix then costs about 2.2 times that of 'plate_clpt_gl'. The points of 'plate_clpt_gl' do not integrate this model exactly but are enough in practice, at about half the cost:

from panels.shell import gauss_points

for p in panels:  # every domain of model 'plate_clpt_tl'
    p.nx = gauss_points(p.m)
    p.ny = gauss_points(p.n)

With them, the internal forces of a plate with displacement gradients of order 0.5 differ by 3e-13, and the tip deflections of the wing box of Large rotations: plates with Green-Lagrange membrane strains under a follower tip force by less than 0.01 mm up to 500 N. Assigning None to nx and ny restores the exact default.

Kinematics#

With \(\boldsymbol{X} = (x, y, 0) + (u, v, w)\) the deformed mid-surface and its area vector \(\boldsymbol{a} = \boldsymbol{X}_{,x} \times \boldsymbol{X}_{,y}\), the strains are

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = u_{,x} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,x}^2 + v_{,x}^2 + w_{,x}^2\right) \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = v_{,y} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,y}^2 + v_{,y}^2 + w_{,y}^2\right)\\\gamma_{xy} = u_{,y} + v_{,x} + u_{,x} u_{,y} + v_{,x} v_{,y} + w_{,x} w_{,y}\\\kappa_{xx} = -\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,xx} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = -\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,yy} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = -2\, \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,xy}\\\boldsymbol{a} = \left( v_{,x} w_{,y} - w_{,x} (1 + v_{,y}),\; w_{,x} u_{,y} - (1 + u_{,x}) w_{,y},\; (1 + u_{,x})(1 + v_{,y}) - v_{,x} u_{,y} \right)\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

with \(\boldsymbol{u}_{,xx} = (u_{,xx}, v_{,xx}, w_{,xx})\) and so on. The linear part is that of 'plate_clpt_donnell', and the quadratic part of \(\kappa_{xx}\) is \(w_{,x} u_{,xx} + w_{,y} v_{,xx} - (u_{,x} + v_{,y}) w_{,xx}\), of the order of the membrane strain times the curvature, so that for moderate deflections the response differs from that of 'plate_clpt_gl' by a relative amount of the order of the membrane strain. For a rotation \(\boldsymbol{X} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{X}\) of a deformed state, \(\boldsymbol{a} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{a}\) and \(\boldsymbol{u}_{,\alpha\beta} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{u}_{,\alpha\beta}\), so the curvatures do not change.

The area vector is used instead of the unit normal \(\boldsymbol{n} = \boldsymbol{a}/|\boldsymbol{a}|\), with \(|\boldsymbol{a}|^2 = \det(\boldsymbol{F}^\top \boldsymbol{F}) = (1 + 2 \varepsilon_{xx})(1 + 2 \varepsilon_{yy}) - \gamma_{xy}^2\): \(|\boldsymbol{a}| = 1\) for any rigid motion, and \(1 + \varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy}\) to first order otherwise. The curvatures are \(|\boldsymbol{a}|\) times those of the unit normal, and the bending stiffness is scaled by \(1 + 2(\varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy})\), an error of the order of the membrane strain, as the other terms neglected by the Kirchhoff-Love kinematics with a constitutive law linear in the Green-Lagrange strains; in exchange the strains are cubic polynomials in the displacements, the integrands polynomials, and the Gauss-Legendre rule is exact.

Internal forces and tangent stiffness#

At a point, the strains depend on the six gradients and the nine second derivatives of \(u, v, w\), \(\boldsymbol{d} = \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{c}\). With \(\boldsymbol{B} = \partial \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}/\partial \boldsymbol{d}\), quadratic in \(\boldsymbol{d}\), and \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d}) = \sum_r \sigma_r\, \partial^2 \varepsilon_r/\partial \boldsymbol{d}^2\), linear in \(\boldsymbol{d}\):

\[\boldsymbol{F}_{int} = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{\sigma}\, dA \qquad \boldsymbol{K}_T = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \left( \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{ABD}\, \boldsymbol{B} + \boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d}) \right) \boldsymbol{\Phi}\, dA\]

Shell.calc_kG() assembles \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_L + \bar{\boldsymbol{N}}, \boldsymbol{0})\), homogeneous of degree one in \(\boldsymbol{c}\) as the linear buckling analysis requires: the u-u, v-v and w-w blocks of 'plate_clpt_gl', and u-w and v-w blocks of the moments of the linear strains. Shell.calc_kC() with NLgeom=True assembles the rest, so that their sum is the Jacobian of Shell.calc_fint(). With a constant membrane stress the geometric stiffness is that of 'plate_clpt_gl', whose analytical kernels the model uses: linear static solutions and natural frequencies are those of 'plate_clpt_donnell', and linear buckling eigenvalues those of 'plate_clpt_gl' when the prebuckling state has no moments. Shell.calc_kTv() evaluates \(\boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{ABD}\, \boldsymbol{B} \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{v} + \boldsymbol{H} \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{v}\) at each point, the strains not having the form of Tangent stiffness matrix and its product by a vector. Follower pressures use the exact area vector of the plate, follower='quadratic', and the connections of MultiDomain are unchanged.

The integrands are polynomials of degree \(6p - 2\) along each direction, for approximation functions of degree \(p = \max(m - 1, 3)\), against \(4p\) for the other models; the default number of points of this model, gauss_points() with the key 'integrand_degree' of panels.modelDB, is \(3p\), 21 for m = 8 against 15, which integrates them exactly. The highest-degree terms are products of five or six of the highest functions and carry little weight: with the 15 points of 'plate_clpt_gl' the internal forces of a state with gradients of order 0.5 differ by 3e-13, so nx = gauss_points(m), ny = gauss_points(n) is a cheaper choice in practice. clpt_field.fstrain() gives the curvatures of the deformed normal.

Accuracy for large rotations#

Benchmark 3.2 of [sze2004Benchmarks], a cantilever strip rolled into a full circle by an end moment, has the exact solution of the inextensible beam, an arc of curvature \(M/EI\). 'plate_clpt_tl' rolls the strip into the circle within about \(2 \times 10^{-3} L\) of that solution; the difference is that of the model, not of the Ritz approximation: with the curvature measured by the area vector, \(\kappa_{xx} = \lambda^2 \mu\, \theta_{,x}\) for the stretches \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) along \(x\) and \(y\), the bending moment shortens the strip by terms of order \((\kappa h)^2\). The angle \(2 \pi\) of the full circle requires polynomials of degree 11 or more along the strip. 'plate_clpt_gl', whose curvatures are measured in the fixed frame, is too flexible from tip rotations of about 0.3 rad, and 'plate_clpt_donnell', whose membrane strains are linear in \(u\), never rolls the strip. Under a tip force of constant direction a cantilever strip bent out of plane follows the inextensible elastica, where 'plate_clpt_gl' follows the linear beam, and bent in its plane, the bending being carried by the membrane strains, it follows the elastica as 'plate_clpt_gl' does.

In the wing box of Large rotations: plates with Green-Lagrange membrane strains, below the buckling of the top skin, the box bends through the membrane strains of the skins and the in-plane bending of the spars, and 'plate_clpt_tl' and 'plate_clpt_gl' agree; above it, 'plate_clpt_tl' is a few per cent stiffer, since the buckles of the top skin, rotated with the box, keep their bending energy instead of losing the factor \(\cos^2\theta\).
