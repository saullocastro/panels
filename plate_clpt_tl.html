Large rotations: total-Lagrangian Kirchhoff-Love plates#

The model 'plate_clpt_tl' is the plate of 'plate_clpt_gl', with the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains, whose changes of curvature are measured with the normal of the deformed mid-surface instead of the fixed local frame. All its strains are then invariant under any rigid rotation of any deformed state: a bending deformation carried through a large rotation, e.g. a local buckle of a skin of a wing box bent by a large rotation, keeps its bending energy, which 'plate_clpt_gl' multiplies by \(\cos^2\theta\). The derivation, with SymPy, is in theory/shells/plate_clpt_tl/plate_clpt_tl.py, with the note plate_clpt_tl.md, and write_pyx.py generates panels.models.plate_clpt_tl_num with the helpers of the generator of 'plate_clpt_gl'.

Note

Integration points: exact by default, a faster option. By default the integrals of 'plate_clpt_tl' are exact, with \(3p\) Gauss-Legendre points along each direction, 21 for m = 8, against the \(2p + 1\), 15, of 'plate_clpt_gl', see Internal forces and tangent stiffness. A tangent stiffness matrix then costs about 2.2 times that of 'plate_clpt_gl'. The points of 'plate_clpt_gl' do not integrate this model exactly but are enough in practice, at about half the cost:

from panels.shell import gauss_points

for p in panels:  # every domain of model 'plate_clpt_tl'
    p.nx = gauss_points(p.m)
    p.ny = gauss_points(p.n)

With them, the internal forces of a plate with displacement gradients of order 0.5 differ by 3e-13, and the tip deflections of the wing box under a follower tip force by less than 0.01 mm up to 500 N, in 138 s against 288 s, see Wing box under a follower tip force. Assigning None to nx and ny restores the exact default.

Kinematics#

With \(\boldsymbol{X} = (x, y, 0) + (u, v, w)\) the deformed mid-surface and its area vector \(\boldsymbol{a} = \boldsymbol{X}_{,x} \times \boldsymbol{X}_{,y}\), the strains are

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = u_{,x} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,x}^2 + v_{,x}^2 + w_{,x}^2\right) \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = v_{,y} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,y}^2 + v_{,y}^2 + w_{,y}^2\right)\\\gamma_{xy} = u_{,y} + v_{,x} + u_{,x} u_{,y} + v_{,x} v_{,y} + w_{,x} w_{,y}\\\kappa_{xx} = -\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,xx} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = -\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,yy} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = -2\, \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{u}_{,xy}\\\boldsymbol{a} = \left( v_{,x} w_{,y} - w_{,x} (1 + v_{,y}),\; w_{,x} u_{,y} - (1 + u_{,x}) w_{,y},\; (1 + u_{,x})(1 + v_{,y}) - v_{,x} u_{,y} \right)\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

with \(\boldsymbol{u}_{,xx} = (u_{,xx}, v_{,xx}, w_{,xx})\) and so on. The linear part is that of 'plate_clpt_donnell', and the quadratic part of \(\kappa_{xx}\) is \(w_{,x} u_{,xx} + w_{,y} v_{,xx} - (u_{,x} + v_{,y}) w_{,xx}\). For a rotation \(\boldsymbol{X} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{X}\) of a deformed state, \(\boldsymbol{a} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{a}\) and \(\boldsymbol{u}_{,\alpha\beta} \to \boldsymbol{R} \boldsymbol{u}_{,\alpha\beta}\), so the curvatures do not change.

The area vector is used instead of the unit normal \(\boldsymbol{n} = \boldsymbol{a}/|\boldsymbol{a}|\), with \(|\boldsymbol{a}|^2 = \det(\boldsymbol{F}^\top \boldsymbol{F}) = (1 + 2 \varepsilon_{xx})(1 + 2 \varepsilon_{yy}) - \gamma_{xy}^2\): \(|\boldsymbol{a}| = 1\) for any rigid motion, and \(1 + \varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy}\) to first order otherwise. The curvatures are \(|\boldsymbol{a}|\) times those of the unit normal, and the bending stiffness is scaled by \(1 + 2(\varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy})\), an error of the order of the membrane strain, as the other terms neglected by the Kirchhoff-Love kinematics with a constitutive law linear in the Green-Lagrange strains; in exchange the strains are cubic polynomials in the displacements, the integrands polynomials, and the Gauss-Legendre rule is exact.

Internal forces and tangent stiffness#

At a point, the strains depend on the six gradients and the nine second derivatives of \(u, v, w\), \(\boldsymbol{d} = \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{c}\). With \(\boldsymbol{B} = \partial \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}/\partial \boldsymbol{d}\), quadratic in \(\boldsymbol{d}\), and \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d}) = \sum_r \sigma_r\, \partial^2 \varepsilon_r/\partial \boldsymbol{d}^2\), linear in \(\boldsymbol{d}\):

\[\boldsymbol{F}_{int} = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{\sigma}\, dA \qquad \boldsymbol{K}_T = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \left( \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{ABD}\, \boldsymbol{B} + \boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d}) \right) \boldsymbol{\Phi}\, dA\]

Shell.calc_kG() assembles \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_L + \bar{\boldsymbol{N}}, \boldsymbol{0})\), homogeneous of degree one in \(\boldsymbol{c}\) as the linear buckling analysis requires: the u-u, v-v and w-w blocks of 'plate_clpt_gl', and u-w and v-w blocks of the moments of the linear strains. Shell.calc_kC() with NLgeom=True assembles the rest, so that their sum is the Jacobian of Shell.calc_fint(). With a constant membrane stress the geometric stiffness is that of 'plate_clpt_gl', whose analytical kernels the model uses: linear static solutions and natural frequencies are those of 'plate_clpt_donnell', and linear buckling eigenvalues those of 'plate_clpt_gl' when the prebuckling state has no moments. Shell.calc_kTv() evaluates \(\boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{ABD}\, \boldsymbol{B} \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{v} + \boldsymbol{H} \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{v}\) at each point, the strains not having the form of Tangent stiffness matrix and its product by a vector. Follower pressures use the exact area vector of the plate, follower='quadratic', and the connections of MultiDomain are unchanged.

The integrands are polynomials of degree \(6p - 2\) along each direction, for approximation functions of degree \(p = \max(m - 1, 3)\), against \(4p\) for the other models; the default number of points of this model, gauss_points() with the key 'integrand_degree' of panels.modelDB, is \(3p\), 21 for m = 8 against 15, which integrates them exactly. The highest-degree terms are products of five or six of the highest functions and carry little weight: with the 15 points of 'plate_clpt_gl' the internal forces of a state with gradients of order 0.5 differ by 3e-13, so nx = gauss_points(m), ny = gauss_points(n) is a cheaper choice in practice. clpt_field.fstrain() gives the curvatures of the deformed normal.

Verification#

tests/tests_shell/test_plate_clpt_tl.py:

  • rigid rotations of 0.1 to 1 rad of a free plate, in plane, out of plane and about an oblique axis, give zero strains, curvatures and internal forces;

  • a bent plate rotated rigidly by 0.5 and 1 rad keeps its strains and its bending energy to 1e-8, whereas the bending energy of 'plate_clpt_gl' is multiplied by \(\cos^2\theta\) (0.77 and 0.29);

  • the tangent stiffness and Shell.calc_kTv() are the central differences of Shell.calc_fint() at a state rotated by 1 rad;

  • a cantilever strip, L = 1, b = 0.04, t = 2e-3, under a tip force of constant direction bent out of plane follows the inextensible elastica to 1e-4 at \(P L^2/EI = 1\) and \(3\) (tip rotations of 0.46 and 1.0 rad), \(w = 0.30172 L\) and \(0.6033 L\), where 'plate_clpt_gl' follows the linear beam, \(w = L/3\) at \(P L^2/EI = 1\); bent in its plane it follows the elastica as 'plate_clpt_gl';

  • for a simply supported plate under pressure with centre deflections of 1.1, 3.4 and 11 times the thickness, the deflection is smaller than that of 'plate_clpt_gl' by 2e-5, 1.1e-4 and 1.3e-3, the order of the membrane strain.

Cantilever strip rolled into a circle by an end moment#

Benchmark 3.2 of [sze2004Benchmarks] (Fig. 3a, p. 1556): a strip clamped at one end, \(L = 12\), \(b = 1\), \(h = 0.1\), \(E = 1.2 \times 10^6\), \(\nu = 0\), under an end moment \(M\) up to \(M_{max} = 2 \pi M_o\), \(M_o = EI/L = 25/3\), which rolls it into a full circle. The exact solution of the inextensible beam (p. 1555) is the arc of curvature \(M/EI\), with the tip at

\[\frac{U}{L} = \frac{\sin\theta}{\theta} - 1, \qquad \frac{W}{L} = \frac{1 - \cos\theta}{\theta}, \qquad \theta = \frac{M L}{E I} = 2 \pi \frac{M}{M_{max}}\]

tabulated as “Exact” in Table 3a (p. 1557). panels has no applied moments; tests/tests_shell/test_plate_clpt_tl_end_moment.py applies the moment as a conservative load of potential \(-M/b \int_0^b \theta(L, y)\, dy\), \(\theta = \operatorname{atan2}(w_{,x}, 1 + u_{,x})\), with its configuration-dependent load vector and load stiffness passed to structsolve.Analysis through calc_fext(c=c), calc_fint(c, inc) and calc_kC(c, inc). 'plate_clpt_tl' rolls the strip into the circle, within 2.3e-3 \(L\) of the exact solution (0.03 at the full circle). The difference is that of the model, not of the Ritz approximation: the curvature measured with the area vector, \(\kappa_{xx} = \lambda^2 \mu\, \theta_{,x}\) for the stretches \(\lambda\) and \(\mu\) along \(x\) and \(y\), makes the bending moment shorten the strip and its width by \((\kappa h)^2/6\) and \((\kappa h)^2/12\), and the curvature larger than \(M/EI\) by about \(5 (\kappa h)^2/6\), 0.23 % at the full circle (\(\kappa h = 0.052\)). The uniform cylindrical solution of these equations, computed in the test, is reproduced to 4e-6 \(L\); the largest difference with it over the five loads converges with the number of terms \(m\) along the length as 2.3e-2 \(L\) (\(m = 8\)), 2.8e-4 \(L\) (10), 3.9e-6 \(L\) (12) and 3.7e-6 \(L\) (14 and 16), the angle \(2 \pi\) of the full circle requiring polynomials of degree 11 or more. 'plate_clpt_gl', whose curvatures \(-w_{,xx}\) are measured in the fixed frame, is 7 % more flexible in \(W\) already at \(0.05 M_{max}\) (a tip rotation of 0.31 rad), and its Newton-Raphson analysis stops at \(0.079 M_{max}\) (0.50 rad, \(W = 4.19\) against 2.93); 'plate_clpt_donnell', whose membrane strains are linear in \(u\), never rolls the strip: \(W\) keeps increasing, to 14.8 at \(M_{max}\).

The two models agree to 0.03 % up to 200 N, below the first buckling load, where the box bends through the membrane strains of the skins and the in-plane bending of the spars. Above it, 'plate_clpt_tl' is up to 2.2 % stiffer, 5.2 % below Abaqus at 500 N against 3.0 %, which is consistent with the buckles of the top skin, rotated with the box, keeping their bending energy instead of losing the factor \(\cos^2\theta\). The remaining difference with Abaqus includes the 2.7 % of the linear range (mid-surface domains, penalty connections), the perfect geometry and the rotation continuity of the connections, linear in the displacements of the fixed frames, which is exact only for small rotations of the joints.

One analysis takes about twice as long as with 'plate_clpt_gl' (288 s against 146 s on one core of a shared machine), from the 21 x 21 integration points of the skins instead of 15 x 15. With the integration points of 'plate_clpt_gl', setting nx = gauss_points(m) and ny = gauss_points(n) on every domain, the tip deflections are the same to 0.01 mm at every load, in 138 s.
