Total-Lagrangian shear deformable models#

The models 'plate_fsdt_tl' and 'cylshell_fsdt_tl' keep the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains of 'plate_fsdt_gl' and 'cylshell_fsdt_gl' (see Shear deformable models with Green-Lagrange membrane strains) and measure the bending and transverse shear strains with a director that rotates with the deformation, as 'plate_clpt_tl' and 'cylshell_clpt_tl' measure the curvatures with the deformed normal. The degrees of freedom of a term are those of the first-order shear deformation theory (FSDT), \(u, v, w, \phi_x, \phi_y\). The models 'plate_tsdt_tl' and 'cylshell_tsdt_tl' extend them to the third-order shear deformation theory (TSDT) of Reddy [reddy1984HigherOrder], [reddy1985Shells], with the same degrees of freedom, see Third-order shear deformation theory.

Moderate rotation kinematics#

The displacement field is that of the moderate rotation theory of Schmidt and Reddy [schmidt1988Refined], Eq. (16), and Palmerio, Reddy and Schmidt [palmerio1990Moderate], Eq. (16), linear through the thickness:

\[\mathbf{u}(z) = \mathbf{u}^{(0)} + z \, \mathbf{u}^{(1)}\]

with the mid-surface displacements \(\mathbf{u}^{(0)} = (u, v, w)\) and the components \(u^{(1)}_\alpha\) and \(u^{(1)}_3\) of the change of the normal. Strains of order \(\epsilon^2\), rotations of the normal of order \(\epsilon\) and rotations about the normal of order \(\epsilon^2\) ([palmerio1990Moderate], Eqs. (9) and (18)) lead to strains of the Green-Lagrange tensor kept to \(O(\epsilon^3)\), Eq. (20) of [palmerio1990Moderate], among which the transverse normal strain

\[E^{(0)}_{33} = u^{(1)}_3 + \frac{1}{2} u^{(1)}_\lambda u^{(1)\lambda}\]

The theory of [schmidt1988Refined] keeps \(u^{(1)}_3\) as an unknown and does not assume that the normal is inextensible (p. 613 there). The models of panels set \(E^{(0)}_{33} = 0\), so that \(u^{(1)}_3 = -(\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)/2\), with \(\psi_\alpha = u^{(1)}_\alpha\), and the deformed position of the point at \(z\) is \(\mathbf{X} + z \mathbf{d}\), with the second-order director

(1)#\[\mathbf{d} = \psi_x \mathbf{e}_x + \psi_y \mathbf{e}_y + \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}(\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)\right) \mathbf{e}_z, \qquad |\mathbf{d}|^2 = 1 + \frac{1}{4}(\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)^2\]

in the local basis of the mid-surface, \(\mathbf{e}_{y,y} = -\mathbf{e}_z/r\), \(\mathbf{e}_{z,y} = \mathbf{e}_y/r\) for the cylinder and \(1/r = 0\) for the plate.

The components \(\psi_\alpha\) follow from the linear part of the transverse shear strain of [palmerio1990Moderate], Eq. (20), \(2 E^{(0)}_{\alpha 3} = \Phi^{(0)}_{\alpha 3} + u^{(1)}_\alpha + \ldots\), with \(\Phi^{(0)}_{\alpha 3} = u_{3,\alpha} + b^\lambda_\alpha u_\lambda\), Eq. (15c). For the cylinder, \(b^y_y = -1/r\) with \(w\) positive outwards, so \(\Phi^{(0)}_{y3} = w_{,y} - v/r\), and the transverse shear strain of the FSDT with the Sanders-Koiter kinematics, \(\gamma_{yz} = \phi_y + w_{,y}\), gives

\[\psi_x = \phi_x, \qquad \psi_y = \phi_y + \frac{v}{r}\]

the rotation of the normal of 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders', whose tangential displacement through the thickness is \((1 + z/r) v + z \phi_y\). For the plate, \(\psi_\alpha = \phi_\alpha\). With \(\psi_y = \phi_y\) the cylinder would not keep a rigid rotation about its axis free of strain, where \(\phi_y = 0\) and \(v/r\) is the rotation.

Strains#

With the tangent vectors of the deformed mid-surface in the local basis,

\[\mathbf{X}_{,x} = (1 + u_{,x}, \; v_{,x}, \; w_{,x}) \qquad \mathbf{X}_{,y} = (u_{,y}, \; 1 + v_{,y} + w/r, \; w_{,y} - v/r)\]

and the derivatives of the director \(\mathbf{d}_{,x} = (\psi_{x,x}, \psi_{y,x}, \psi_{z,x})\), \(\mathbf{d}_{,y} = (\psi_{x,y}, \psi_{y,y} + \psi_z/r, \psi_{z,y} - \psi_y/r)\), \(\psi_z = 1 - (\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)/2\), the strains are

(2)#\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{X}_{,x} - 1) \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = \tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{X}_{,y} - 1) \qquad \gamma_{xy} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{X}_{,y}\\\kappa_{xx} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,x} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y} - \frac{1}{r} - \frac{\varepsilon_{yy}}{r} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y} + \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,x} - \frac{\gamma_{xy}}{2 r}\\\gamma_{yz} = \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d} \qquad \gamma_{xz} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

cubic polynomials of the derivatives of the displacements and rotations. Without the terms \(-\varepsilon_{yy}/r\) and \(-\gamma_{xy}/(2r)\) these are the coefficients of \(z^0\) and \(z\) of the Green-Lagrange strains of the field \(\mathbf{X} + z \mathbf{d}\), with the reference vectors \(\mathbf{e}_x\) and \((1 + z/r) \mathbf{e}_y\), and the transverse components at the mid-surface. The two terms, Koiter’s modification of the changes of curvature [vanderheijden2008Koiter], p. 202, also used by 'cylshell_clpt_tl' (see Large rotations: total-Lagrangian Kirchhoff-Love cylindrical shells), make the linear part exactly that of 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders', as follows.

Linear part#

With \(\psi_y = \phi_y + v/r\), \(\psi_z = 1 + O(\psi^2)\), and keeping the terms linear in the displacements and rotations, the dot products of (2) are

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,x} \approx \phi_{x,x} \qquad \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y} - \frac{1}{r} \approx \psi_{y,y} + \frac{1}{r}\left(v_{,y} + \frac{w}{r}\right) = \phi_{y,y} + \frac{v_{,y}}{r} + \frac{\varepsilon^L_{yy}}{r}\\\mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y} \approx \phi_{x,y} + \frac{v_{,x}}{r} \qquad \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,x} \approx \psi_{y,x} = \phi_{y,x} + \frac{v_{,x}}{r}\\\mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d} \approx \psi_y + w_{,y} - \frac{v}{r} = \phi_y + w_{,y} \qquad \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d} \approx \phi_x + w_{,x}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

with \(\varepsilon^L_{yy} = v_{,y} + w/r\) and \(\gamma^L_{xy} = u_{,y} + v_{,x}\) the linear membrane strains. In \(\mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y}\) the reference \(1/r\) and the hoop strain come from \((1 + v_{,y} + w/r) \psi_z/r\); in \(\mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}_{,y}\) the term \(v_{,x}/r\) comes from \(v_{,x} \psi_z/r\). Without the two Koiter terms the linear curvatures are those of Sanders plus \((0, \varepsilon^L_{yy}/r, \gamma^L_{xy}/(2r))\); with them

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\kappa^L_{xx} = \phi_{x,x} \qquad \kappa^L_{yy} = \phi_{y,y} + \frac{v_{,y}}{r} \qquad \kappa^L_{xy} = \phi_{x,y} + \phi_{y,x} + \frac{3}{2} \frac{v_{,x}}{r} - \frac{1}{2} \frac{u_{,y}}{r}\\\gamma^L_{yz} = \phi_y + w_{,y} \qquad \gamma^L_{xz} = \phi_x + w_{,x}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

the linear strains of 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders', whose twist contains the rotation about the normal, \((v_{,x} - u_{,y})/(2r)\). The symbolic check check_linear_part of the generator of 'cylshell_fsdt_tl' verifies each of these steps and the equality of the linear strain-displacement operator with that of 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders'. The Koiter terms use the complete Green-Lagrange strains, which a rigid rotation does not change. For the plate, \(1/r = 0\), the linear part is that of 'plate_fsdt_donnell' (check_linear_part of 'plate_fsdt_tl').

Dropped terms#

As in every FSDT model of panels, the coefficient of \(z^2\) of the in-plane strains is dropped, and so is the coefficient of \(z\) of the transverse shear strains, \(\mathbf{d}_{,\alpha} \cdot \mathbf{d} = (\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)(\psi_x \psi_{x,\alpha} + \psi_y \psi_{y,\alpha})/2\), of the fourth order.

Comparison with the moderate rotation theory#

With the orders of magnitude of [palmerio1990Moderate], the strains (2) without the two Koiter terms are those of Eq. (20) there, with \(E^{(0)}_{33} = 0\), to \(O(\epsilon^3)\): the differences are of the fourth order, e.g. the quadratic terms of the in-plane gradients in the membrane strains, \(u_{,x} \phi_{x,x}\) in \(\kappa_{xx}\) and \(u^{(1)}_3 (w_{,x} + \phi_x)/2\) in \(\gamma_{xz}\). For the plate the non-linear terms of the curvatures, among them the cubic terms \(-w_{,x} (\phi_x \phi_{x,x} + \phi_y \phi_{y,x})\) from the third component of the director, are all of the fourth order once the normal is inextensible, and the model differs from 'plate_fsdt_gl' at \(O(\epsilon^3)\) through the transverse shear strains only. For the cylinder the curvatures contain quadratic terms of the order of the linear ones, from \(\Phi^{(0)}_{y3} \Phi^{(1)}_{y3}\) with \(\Phi^{(1)}_{y3} = u^{(1)}_{3,y} - \psi_y/r\), which the linear curvatures of 'cylshell_fsdt_gl' lack. The terms beyond \(\epsilon^3\) are kept, so that the strains are exact for the field \(\mathbf{X} + z \mathbf{d}\), built from dot products of vectors that a rigid rotation turns together.

Rigid rotations#

Under a rigid rotation \(\boldsymbol{R}\) of a deformed state the vectors \(\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha}\) turn without change of their dot products, and the membrane strains do not change for any angle. The director (1) cannot follow the rotation exactly: with \(\psi\) the components of the rotated director, its third component differs from that of the unit vector by \((\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)^2/8 + \ldots\), of the fourth order in the angle \(t\). A rigid rotation of the flat plate, with the rotations those of the rotated normal, gives zero membrane and bending strains and transverse shear strains of \(O(t^5)\); a rotation of a bent plate or of the cylinder changes the curvatures and transverse shear strains by \(O(t^4)\), against \(O(t^2)\), the factor \(\cos t\), for the linear curvatures of the GL models.

Third-order shear deformation theory#

Reddy’s displacement field [reddy1984HigherOrder] adds to the field of the FSDT a cubic term that makes the transverse shear strains vanish at the faces \(z = \pm h/2\):

\[\mathbf{u}(z) = \mathbf{u} + z (\phi_x, \phi_y, 0) - c_1 z^3 (\phi_x + w_{,x}, \; \phi_y + w_{,y}, \; 0), \qquad c_1 = \frac{4}{3 h^2}\]

For finite rotations, the vector of the cubic term, \((\phi_x + w_{,x}, \phi_y + w_{,y}, 0)\), is read as the linear part of the difference between the director (1) and the normal of the deformed mid-surface, not normalized,

\[\mathbf{N} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \times \mathbf{X}_{,y}\]

whose linear part is \((-w_{,x}, -w_{,y} + v/r, 1 + u_{,x} + v_{,y} + w/r)\) in the local basis. The deformed position of the point at \(z\) of the models 'plate_tsdt_tl' and 'cylshell_tsdt_tl' is

(3)#\[\mathbf{r}(z) = \mathbf{X} + z \mathbf{d} + z^3 \mathbf{q}, \qquad \mathbf{q} = -c_1 (\mathbf{d} - \mathbf{N})\]

Its strains are the coefficients of \(z^0\), \(z\) and \(z^3\) of the in-plane Green-Lagrange strains and of \(z^0\) and \(z^2\) of the transverse shear strains, the powers of the linear TSDT: the strains (2) and

(4)#\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\kappa^{(3)}_{xx} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{,x} \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{yy} = \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{,y} - \frac{c_1}{r} (\varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy}) \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{xy} = \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{,y} + \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{,x}\\\gamma^{(2)}_{yz} = 3 \mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{q} \qquad \gamma^{(2)}_{xz} = 3 \mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{q}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

with the derivatives of the vectors along \(y\) in the local basis of the cylinder as for \(\mathbf{d}_{,y}\), and \(1/r = 0\) for the plate. Dropped, as in the linear TSDT: the coefficients of \(z^2\), \(z^4\) and \(z^6\) of the in-plane strains, of the odd powers of the transverse shear strains and the transverse normal strain. The second derivatives of \(u\) and \(v\) enter through those of \(\mathbf{X}\).

Since \(\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha} \cdot \mathbf{N} = 0\) for any deformation, \(\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha} \cdot \mathbf{N}_{,\beta} = -\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha\beta} \cdot \mathbf{N}\) and, for the plate,

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\kappa^{(3)}_{xx} = -c_1 (\kappa_{xx} + \mathbf{X}_{,xx} \cdot \mathbf{N}) \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{yy} = -c_1 (\kappa_{yy} + \mathbf{X}_{,yy} \cdot \mathbf{N}) \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{xy} = -c_1 (\kappa_{xy} + 2 \mathbf{X}_{,xy} \cdot \mathbf{N})\\\gamma^{(2)}_\alpha = -3 c_1 \gamma_\alpha\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

Reddy’s \(\phi_{\alpha,\beta}\) becomes the curvature of the director and \(w_{,\alpha\beta}\) the second fundamental form of the deformed mid-surface, \(\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha\beta} \cdot \mathbf{N}\). The relation \(\gamma^{(2)} = -3 c_1 \gamma\) is exact, so that the transverse shear strain through the thickness, \(\gamma_\alpha (1 - 4 z^2/h^2)\), vanishes at the faces for any deformation: Reddy’s traction-free faces hold at finite rotations.

Cylinder#

The \(z\) component of \(\mathbf{N}\) is \(1 + \varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy}\) to first order, the stretch of the area of the mid-surface. In the plate it is dropped with the coefficient of \(z^3\) of the transverse shear strains, whose linear part it is, \(c_1 (u_{,x} + v_{,y})_{,\alpha}\). In the cylinder it enters \(\mathbf{q}_{,y}\) through the curvature of the reference, \(\mathbf{e}_{z,y} = \mathbf{e}_y/r\), and adds \(c_1 (u_{,x} + v_{,y} + w/r)/r\) to the linear part of \(\mathbf{X}_{,y} \cdot \mathbf{q}_{,y}\). The term \(-c_1 (\varepsilon_{xx} + \varepsilon_{yy})/r\) of (4), with the Green-Lagrange membrane strains, removes it, as the Koiter terms of (2) remove the membrane strains from the linear curvatures; the linear part is then that of 'cylshell_tsdt_sanders', \(\kappa^{(3)}_{xx} = -c_1 (\phi_{x,x} + w_{,xx})\), \(\kappa^{(3)}_{yy} = -c_1 (\phi_{y,y} + w_{,yy})\), \(\kappa^{(3)}_{xy} = -c_1 (\phi_{x,y} + \phi_{y,x} + 2 w_{,xy})\), and the linear parts of 'plate_tsdt_tl' and 'cylshell_tsdt_tl' are those of 'plate_tsdt_donnell' and 'cylshell_tsdt_sanders'.

Rigid rotations#

The strains (4) are dot products of vectors that a rigid rotation turns together, and Green-Lagrange membrane strains; as for the FSDT, only the second-order director cannot follow the rotation: a rigid rotation of the flat plate gives zero membrane, bending and third-order bending strains and transverse shear strains of \(O(t^5)\), and a rotation of a bent plate or of the cylinder changes the other strains by \(O(t^4)\). The linear third-order strains of 'plate_tsdt_gl' and 'cylshell_tsdt_gl' change at the second or the first order in the angle.

Third-order terms linear#

A simpler model keeps the first-order part (2) and the linear third-order strains of Reddy, those of the GL models, \(\kappa^{(3)}_{\alpha\beta} = -c_1 (\phi_{\alpha,\beta} + \phi_{\beta,\alpha} + 2 w_{,\alpha\beta})/(1 + \delta_{\alpha\beta})\) and \(\gamma^{(2)}_\alpha = -3 c_1 (\phi_\alpha + w_{,\alpha})\), as Amabili [amabili2018Nonlinear], Sec. 3.9, does in a model with the nonlinear terms of von Kármán. It has the same linear part, but its shear strain at the faces is \(\mathbf{X}_{,\alpha} \cdot \mathbf{d} - (\phi_\alpha + w_{,\alpha})\), cubic, not zero, and a rigid rotation about an oblique axis gives third-order strains of \(O(t^2)\). In large bending the shear strain of the director, \(\mathbf{X}_{,x} \cdot \mathbf{d}\), and the linear one, \(\phi_x + w_{,x}\), cannot both vanish: with \(\gamma\) and \(\gamma_L\) these two strains, the transverse shear energy per unit area of an isotropic layer is \(G h (\gamma^2 - \tfrac{2}{3} \gamma \gamma_L + \tfrac{1}{5} \gamma_L^2)/2\), which penalizes the bending by a factor of the order of \((L/h)^2\): the model locks. A strip of \(L/h = 500\), clamped at one end under a tip force of constant direction normal to its plane, \(\alpha = P L^2/EI = 1\), whose inextensible elastica has the tip rotation 0.46 rad, \(w = 0.3017 L\) and \(-u = 0.0564 L\), gives with that model \(w\) 25.5 % and \(-u\) 47 % too small, and at \(\alpha = 3\) (0.99 rad) 50 % and 79 %; the field (3) gives -0.22 % and -0.47 % at \(\alpha = 1\), -1.4 % and -3.8 % at \(\alpha = 3\), close to the FSDT model (-0.27 %, -0.59 %; -1.8 %, -4.7 %), the differences coming from the second-order director. A thick strip, \(L/h = 5\), compared with the geometrically exact beam of Reissner [reissner1972Beam] with the shear stiffness of its linear deflection, at \(\alpha = 3\) (0.98 rad): -3.4 % and -7.9 % for the linear third-order terms, -0.81 % and -0.20 % for (3). Schmidt and Reddy [schmidt1988Refined], Eqs. (17)-(19), written for an expansion of any order in the thickness coordinate, have products of the rotation terms of all orders in the strains, i.e. third-order strains that are not linear.

The field (3) costs more: 21 generalized derivatives at an integration point of the plate instead of 15, and for a plate of 12 x 12 terms the constitutive and geometric stiffness matrices take about 1.3 and 1.7 times as long as with the linear third-order terms.

Tangent stiffness#

At an integration point the strains depend on the generalized derivatives \(\boldsymbol{d} = \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{c}\) of the GL models: the gradients of \(u, v, w\), for the cylinder the values of \(v\) and \(w\), the rotations and their gradients, and for the TSDT the second derivatives of \(u\), \(v\) and \(w\). With \(\boldsymbol{B} = \partial \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}/\partial \boldsymbol{d}\), quadratic in \(\boldsymbol{d}\), and the generalized stresses \(\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \boldsymbol{F}_c \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\):

\[\boldsymbol{f}_{int} = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{\sigma} \, dA \qquad \boldsymbol{K}_T = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \left(\boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{F}_c \boldsymbol{B} + \boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d})\right) \boldsymbol{\Phi} \, dA, \qquad \boldsymbol{H} = \sum_r \sigma_r \frac{\partial^2 \varepsilon_r}{\partial \boldsymbol{d}^2}\]

\(\boldsymbol{H}\) is linear in \(\boldsymbol{d}\) and involves the moments and transverse shear forces as well as the membrane forces. The geometric stiffness of linear buckling is \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}_L, \boldsymbol{0})\), homogeneous in the prebuckling state; with a constant membrane state only it is that of the GL models, whose analytical kernels the two models use for linear statics, vibration and linear buckling. The integrands of \(\boldsymbol{f}_{int}\) and \(\boldsymbol{K}_T\) are polynomials of degree \(6 p\) along each direction, \(p\) the degree of the approximation functions (the rotations enter without derivative), integrated exactly by \(3 p + 1\) Gauss-Legendre points, the default of these models.

Limitations#

  • Moderate rotations: the director has the length \((1 + (\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2)^2/4)^{1/2}\), with \(\psi_x^2 + \psi_y^2 = \sin^2 t\) for a normal rotated by \(t\): 0.7 % too long at 0.5 rad, 6 % at 1 rad.

  • The thickness does not change and the constitutive law is linear in the Green-Lagrange strains (small strains).

  • The connections of MultiDomain stay linear in the local displacements and rotations.

  • The third-order shear deformation theory keeps the transverse shear strain of the director through the thickness, \(\gamma_\alpha (1 - 4 z^2/h^2)\), and drops the higher powers of the thickness coordinate, as the linear TSDT.
