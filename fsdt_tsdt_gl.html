Shear deformable models with Green-Lagrange membrane strains#

The models 'plate_fsdt_gl', 'plate_tsdt_gl', 'cylshell_fsdt_gl' and 'cylshell_tsdt_gl' combine the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains of 'plate_clpt_gl' and 'cylshell_clpt_gl' (see Large rotations: plates with Green-Lagrange membrane strains) with the first-order (FSDT) and third-order (TSDT) shear deformation theories of 'plate_fsdt_donnell', 'plate_tsdt_donnell', 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders' and 'cylshell_tsdt_sanders'. The degrees of freedom of a term are those of the shear deformation theories, \(u, v, w, \phi_x, \phi_y\).

Strains#

The membrane strains are the Green-Lagrange strains of the mid-surface, \(\boldsymbol{E} = (\boldsymbol{F}^\top \boldsymbol{F} - \boldsymbol{I})/2\), with the columns of \(\boldsymbol{F}\) the tangent vectors of the deformed mid-surface in the local basis. For the cylinder of radius \(r\), \(y\) the arc length and \(w\) positive outwards:

(1)#\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = u_{,x} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,x}^2 + v_{,x}^2 + w_{,x}^2\right)\\\varepsilon_{yy} = v_{,y} + \frac{w}{r} + \tfrac{1}{2}\left(u_{,y}^2 + \left(v_{,y} + \frac{w}{r}\right)^2 + \left(w_{,y} - \frac{v}{r}\right)^2\right)\\\gamma_{xy} = u_{,y} + v_{,x} + u_{,x} u_{,y} + v_{,x}\left(v_{,y} + \frac{w}{r}\right) + w_{,x}\left(w_{,y} - \frac{v}{r}\right)\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

those of the Flügge-Lur’e-Byrne non-linear theory as given by Amabili [amabili2008Nonlinear], Eqs. (1.118)-(1.120), and \(1/r = 0\) for the plates. The bending and transverse shear strains are the linear ones of the FSDT, with the terms of Sanders [sanders1963Nonlinear] for the cylinders:

(2)#\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\kappa_{xx} = \phi_{x,x} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = \phi_{y,y} + \frac{v_{,y}}{r} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = \phi_{x,y} + \phi_{y,x} + \frac{3}{2} \frac{v_{,x}}{r} - \frac{1}{2} \frac{u_{,y}}{r}\\\gamma_{yz} = \phi_y + w_{,y} \qquad \gamma_{xz} = \phi_x + w_{,x}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

and, for the TSDT of Reddy [reddy1984HigherOrder], the third-order terms \(-c_1 (\phi_{x,x} + w_{,xx})\), \(-c_1 (\phi_{y,y} + w_{,yy})\), \(-c_1 (\phi_{x,y} + \phi_{y,x} + 2 w_{,xy})\) of the in-plane strains and \(-3 c_1 \gamma_{yz}\), \(-3 c_1 \gamma_{xz}\) of the transverse shear strains, \(c_1 = 4/(3 h^2)\), as in Third-order shear deformation theory with a laminate offset.

Relation with the other models#

Without the quadratic terms the strains are those of 'plate_fsdt_donnell', 'plate_tsdt_donnell', 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders' and 'cylshell_tsdt_sanders': the linear constitutive stiffness, the mass and the aerodynamic matrices are theirs, and so are linear static solutions and natural frequencies. Their non-linear terms, the squares of the rotations \(\beta_x = -w_{,x}\), \(\beta_y = -w_{,y} + v/r\), are contained in Eq. (1), which adds the quadratic terms of the in-plane gradients \(u_{,x}\), \(u_{,y}\), \(v_{,x}\) and \(v_{,y} + w/r\); the geometric stiffness has blocks \(u-u\) and \(v-v\) (and \(v-w\) for the cylinders) besides the block \(w-w\). Under an axial membrane force the term \(N_{xx} v_{,x}^2\) is the one that gives the Euler load of a tube bending as a beam, as for 'cylshell_clpt_gl'.

Rigid rotations#

For a finite rigid rotation \(\boldsymbol{R}\) the tangent vectors are rotated without stretch, \(\boldsymbol{F}^\top \boldsymbol{F} = \boldsymbol{I}\), and the membrane strains vanish for any angle and axis. For the plates the displacement gradients are constant, the first two columns of \(\boldsymbol{R} - \boldsymbol{I}\); with the constant rotations \(\phi_x = -w_{,x}\), \(\phi_y = -w_{,y}\) the bending, third-order and transverse shear strains vanish too, and a rigid rotation of any size stores no energy. For the cylinders the linear bending strains of a finite rigid rotation about an axis normal to the cylinder axis are of the order \((1 - \cos t)/r\), as for 'cylshell_clpt_gl'. A bending deformation superposed on a large rotation \(t\) about an in-plane axis is seen with the factor \(\cos t\), the bending strains being measured in the fixed local basis.

Tangent stiffness#

At an integration point the strains \(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\) depend on the generalized derivatives \(\boldsymbol{d} = \boldsymbol{\Phi} \boldsymbol{c}\): the gradients of \(u, v, w\), for the cylinders the values of \(v\) and \(w\), the rotations and their gradients and, for the TSDT, the second derivatives of \(w\). With \(\boldsymbol{B} = \partial \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}/\partial \boldsymbol{d}\), the generalized stresses \(\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \boldsymbol{F}_c \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}\) (\(\boldsymbol{F}_c\) the constitutive matrix of the FSDT or TSDT) and the six local gradients \(\boldsymbol{\beta} = \boldsymbol{G}_m \boldsymbol{d}\) of Eq. (1):

\[\boldsymbol{f}_{int} = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{\sigma} \, dA \qquad \boldsymbol{K}_T = \int \boldsymbol{\Phi}^\top \left(\boldsymbol{B}^\top \boldsymbol{F}_c \boldsymbol{B} + \boldsymbol{G}_m^\top \mathrm{diag}(\boldsymbol{N}, \boldsymbol{N}, \boldsymbol{N}) \boldsymbol{G}_m\right) \boldsymbol{\Phi} \, dA\]

with \(\boldsymbol{N}\) the \(2 \times 2\) matrix of the membrane stresses. Only the membrane strains are non-linear and quadratic, so \(\boldsymbol{B}\) is linear in \(\boldsymbol{d}\) and the integrands are products of four approximation functions, integrated exactly by the default Gauss-Legendre rule of the linear models.

Shear deformation and total-Lagrangian kinematics#

With independent rotations the total-Lagrangian kinematics of a shell rotate the director through finite angles: in the degenerate-continuum shell elements the director is a unit vector defined by two angles, and the strains are the Green-Lagrange strains of the shell continuum [crisfield1991Nonlinear], Sec. 8.2. The rotations of these models are not finite rotations of the director; their bending and transverse shear strains stay linear. The total-Lagrangian FSDT models with the second-order director of the moderate rotation theory are described in Total-Lagrangian shear deformable models.
