Annular-sector plates#

The models 'annular_clpt_donnell', 'annular_clpt_gl' and 'annular_clpt_tl' are flat annular sectors between the outer radius \(r\) and the inner radius \(r - a\), with the classical laminated plate theory (CLPT) in polar coordinates [amabili2008Nonlinear], Sec. 1.4: the von Kármán strains, the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains, and these with the changes of curvature of the deformed normal. They are the polar counterparts of 'plate_clpt_donnell', 'plate_clpt_gl' and 'plate_clpt_tl'. The web of a ring frame of a stiffened cylinder, in the plane normal to the axis and meeting the skin at its outer radius, is an application. The kernels panels.models.annular_clpt_donnell, panels.models.annular_clpt_gl, panels.models.annular_clpt_tl and their numerically integrated modules *_num are generated from symbolic derivations with SymPy of the strains below. The models 'annular_fsdt_donnell', 'annular_fsdt_gl' and 'annular_fsdt_tl' are the same sectors with the first-order shear deformation theory (FSDT), the polar counterparts of 'plate_fsdt_donnell', 'plate_fsdt_gl' and 'plate_fsdt_tl', see First-order shear deformation theory.

Geometry and coordinates#

An annular sector is a Shell with a the radial width, b the arc length of the outer edge and r the outer radius; for the web of a ring frame, a is the height of the web. With the polar coordinates \((\rho, \theta)\) of the plane, the coordinates of the Ritz series of Eq. (1) are

\[x = r - \rho, \quad 0 \le x \le a \qquad\qquad y = r \theta, \quad 0 \le y \le b\]

\(x\) runs from the outer edge inwards and \(y\) along the outer edge. The displacements \(u, v, w\) are measured along the local basis

\[\boldsymbol{e}_x = -\boldsymbol{e}_\rho, \qquad \boldsymbol{e}_y = \boldsymbol{e}_\theta, \qquad \boldsymbol{e}_z = \boldsymbol{e}_x \times \boldsymbol{e}_y\]

which rotates with \(\theta\). In the notation of Amabili, with the radial, circumferential and normal displacements \(u_1, u_2, u_3\) along \(\boldsymbol{e}_\rho\), \(\boldsymbol{e}_\theta\) and \(\boldsymbol{e}_\rho \times \boldsymbol{e}_\theta = -\boldsymbol{e}_z\):

\[u_1 = -u, \quad u_2 = v, \quad u_3 = -w, \qquad \frac{\partial}{\partial \rho} = -\frac{\partial}{\partial x}, \quad \frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} = r \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\]

Strains#

With \(s = r/\rho\) the ratio of the arc length along the outer edge to the arc length at the radius \(\rho\), the Kirchhoff displacement field through the thickness is \(u(z) = u - z w_{,x}\), \(v(z) = v - z s w_{,y}\), \(w(z) = w\). The von Kármán strains of 'annular_clpt_donnell', Eqs. (1.175)-(1.180) of [amabili2008Nonlinear], become

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = u_{,x} + \tfrac{1}{2} w_{,x}^2 \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = s v_{,y} - \frac{u}{\rho} + \tfrac{1}{2} s^2 w_{,y}^2 \qquad \gamma_{xy} = s u_{,y} + v_{,x} + \frac{v}{\rho} + s w_{,x} w_{,y}\\\kappa_{xx} = -w_{,xx} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = \frac{w_{,x}}{\rho} - s^2 w_{,yy} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = -2 s \left(w_{,xy} + \frac{w_{,y}}{\rho}\right)\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

with \(\varepsilon_{xx} = \varepsilon_\rho\), \(\varepsilon_{yy} = \varepsilon_\theta\), \(\gamma_{xy} = -\gamma_{\rho\theta}\) and the curvatures of opposite sign to Amabili’s, the axis \(\boldsymbol{e}_z\) being opposite to his. The terms \(-u/\rho\) and \(v/\rho\) are those of the stretching of a circumferential fibre that moves radially and of the rotation of the local basis; \(w_{,x}/\rho\) is the curvature of a circumferential fibre whose radial slope changes. The six small rigid-body motions of the plane give zero linear strains, and for \(r \to \infty\) the strains are those of 'plate_clpt_donnell'. A flat plate has no curvature term, and Donnell’s shallow-shell strains reduce to von Kármán’s [amabili2008Nonlinear], Sec. 1.3.10: the name follows 'plate_clpt_donnell'.

The deformed reference plane has, per unit length of the reference lines, the tangent vectors

\[\begin{split}\boldsymbol{g}_1 = \boldsymbol{X}_{,x} = \begin{Bmatrix} 1 + u_{,x} \\ v_{,x} \\ w_{,x} \end{Bmatrix}, \qquad \boldsymbol{g}_2 = s \boldsymbol{X}_{,y} = \begin{Bmatrix} s u_{,y} + v/\rho \\ 1 + s v_{,y} - u/\rho \\ s w_{,y} \end{Bmatrix}\end{split}\]

in the local basis. 'annular_clpt_gl' has the complete Green-Lagrange membrane strains, Eqs. (1.166)-(1.168) of [amabili2008Nonlinear],

\[\varepsilon_{xx} = \tfrac{1}{2} (\boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_1 - 1) \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = \tfrac{1}{2} (\boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_2 - 1) \qquad \gamma_{xy} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_2\]

which vanish for any finite rigid motion, and the linear changes of curvature above. 'annular_clpt_tl' also measures the changes of curvature with the area vector \(\boldsymbol{A} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \times \boldsymbol{g}_2\) of the deformed plane, as 'plate_clpt_tl' does:

\[\kappa_{xx} = -\boldsymbol{A} \cdot \boldsymbol{X}_{,xx} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = -s^2 \boldsymbol{A} \cdot \boldsymbol{X}_{,yy} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = -2 s \boldsymbol{A} \cdot \boldsymbol{X}_{,xy}\]

with the second derivatives of the position, in the local basis,

\[\begin{split}\boldsymbol{X}_{,xx} = \begin{Bmatrix} u_{,xx} \\ v_{,xx} \\ w_{,xx} \end{Bmatrix}, \quad \boldsymbol{X}_{,xy} = \begin{Bmatrix} u_{,xy} + v_{,x}/r \\ v_{,xy} - (1 + u_{,x})/r \\ w_{,xy} \end{Bmatrix}, \quad \boldsymbol{X}_{,yy} = \begin{Bmatrix} u_{,yy} + 2 v_{,y}/r + (\rho - u)/r^2 \\ v_{,yy} - 2 u_{,y}/r - v/r^2 \\ w_{,yy} \end{Bmatrix}\end{split}\]

The terms without displacement are those of the flat reference plane in polar coordinates, normal to \(\boldsymbol{A}\) of the undeformed plane. All six strains vanish for any finite rigid motion; their linear part is that of the von Kármán strains. For \(r \to \infty\) the strains of 'annular_clpt_gl' and 'annular_clpt_tl' are those of 'plate_clpt_gl' and 'plate_clpt_tl'. The element of area is

\[dA = \rho \, d\rho \, d\theta = \frac{\rho}{r} \, dx \, dy\]

The constitutive law, the internal force vector and the split of the tangent stiffness into fkC_num and fkG_num are those of the flat plates; the geometric stiffness of a constant membrane state \(N_{xx} = N_{\rho\rho}\), \(N_{yy} = N_{\theta\theta}\), \(N_{xy} = -N_{\rho\theta}\) is the variation of its work on the second-order terms of the membrane strains, on \((w_{,x}, s w_{,y})\) for 'annular_clpt_donnell' and on all the displacement gradients for 'annular_clpt_gl' and 'annular_clpt_tl'.

First-order shear deformation theory#

The models 'annular_fsdt_donnell', 'annular_fsdt_gl' and 'annular_fsdt_tl' have the geometry, the coordinates and the local basis above, with the rotations \(\phi_x\), \(\phi_y\) of the normal as independent fields: five degrees of freedom \(u, v, w, \phi_x, \phi_y\) per term and the displacement field

\[u(z) = u + z \phi_x, \qquad v(z) = v + z \phi_y, \qquad w(z) = w\]

of 'plate_fsdt_donnell'. The von Kármán strains of the Mindlin plate in polar coordinates [bagheri2019Torque], Eqs. (2) and (6), are those of 'annular_fsdt_donnell':

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = u_{,x} + \tfrac{1}{2} w_{,x}^2 \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = s v_{,y} - \frac{u}{\rho} + \tfrac{1}{2} s^2 w_{,y}^2 \qquad \gamma_{xy} = s u_{,y} + v_{,x} + \frac{v}{\rho} + s w_{,x} w_{,y}\\\kappa_{xx} = \phi_{x,x} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = s \phi_{y,y} - \frac{\phi_x}{\rho} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = s \phi_{x,y} + \phi_{y,x} + \frac{\phi_y}{\rho}\\\gamma_{yz} = \phi_y + s w_{,y} \qquad \gamma_{xz} = \phi_x + w_{,x}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

In the notation of [bagheri2019Torque], whose \(z\) is along \(-\boldsymbol{e}_z\), the radial and circumferential displacements and rotations are \(u_0 = -u\), \(v_0 = v\), \(w_0 = -w\), \(\psi_r = \phi_x\) and \(\psi_\theta = -\phi_y\). The terms \(-\phi_x/\rho\) and \(\phi_y/\rho\) are those of \(-u/\rho\) and \(v/\rho\) for the displacements \(z \phi_x\), \(z \phi_y\) of the points off the reference plane. With \(\phi_x = -w_{,x}\) and \(\phi_y = -s w_{,y}\) the transverse shear strains vanish and the changes of curvature are those of 'annular_clpt_donnell'.

'annular_fsdt_gl' has the Green-Lagrange membrane strains of 'annular_clpt_gl' and the linear changes of curvature and transverse shear strains above, as 'plate_fsdt_gl' for the flat plate. 'annular_fsdt_tl' is total-Lagrangian with the second-order director of 'plate_fsdt_tl' [schmidt1988Refined], [palmerio1990Moderate]: the deformed position of a point is \(\boldsymbol{X} + z \boldsymbol{d}\), with

\[\boldsymbol{d} = \phi_x \boldsymbol{e}_x + \phi_y \boldsymbol{e}_y + \left(1 - \tfrac{1}{2} (\phi_x^2 + \phi_y^2)\right) \boldsymbol{e}_z\]

and, the local basis turning with \(\theta\) (\(\boldsymbol{e}_{x,y} = -\boldsymbol{e}_y/r\), \(\boldsymbol{e}_{y,y} = \boldsymbol{e}_x/r\)), the derivatives of the director per unit length of the reference lines

\[\begin{split}\boldsymbol{d}_{,x} = \begin{Bmatrix} \phi_{x,x} \\ \phi_{y,x} \\ -(\phi_x \phi_{x,x} + \phi_y \phi_{y,x}) \end{Bmatrix}, \qquad s \boldsymbol{d}_{,y} = \begin{Bmatrix} s \phi_{x,y} + \phi_y/\rho \\ s \phi_{y,y} - \phi_x/\rho \\ -s (\phi_x \phi_{x,y} + \phi_y \phi_{y,y}) \end{Bmatrix}\end{split}\]

The strains are the coefficients of \(z^0\) and \(z^1\) of the Green-Lagrange strains of this field, the metric of the flat reference plane not depending on \(z\):

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\varepsilon_{xx} = \tfrac{1}{2} (\boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_1 - 1) \qquad \varepsilon_{yy} = \tfrac{1}{2} (\boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_2 - 1) \qquad \gamma_{xy} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{g}_2\\\kappa_{xx} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{d}_{,x} \qquad \kappa_{yy} = \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot s \boldsymbol{d}_{,y} \qquad \kappa_{xy} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot s \boldsymbol{d}_{,y} + \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{d}_{,x}\\\gamma_{yz} = \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{d} \qquad \gamma_{xz} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{d}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

Their linear part is that of 'annular_fsdt_donnell': the reference plane has no curvature of its own, and no correction of the linear part is needed, unlike for the cylinder of Total-Lagrangian shear deformable models. A finite rigid rotation by the angle \(t\) changes the strains by \(O(t^4)\), through the length of the second-order director; the linear transverse shear strains of 'annular_fsdt_gl' change by \(O(t^2)\) under a rotation about an axis inclined to the plane. For \(r \to \infty\) the strains of the three models are those of 'plate_fsdt_donnell', 'plate_fsdt_gl' and 'plate_fsdt_tl'.

The constitutive matrix Shell.ABD of these models is the \(8 \times 8\) matrix of the FSDT, with the transverse shear stiffness and the shear correction chosen with Shell.fsdt_shear_correction; the mass matrix has the moments \(I_0, I_1, I_2\) of the density with the displacement field above. The transverse shear lowers the buckling loads and frequencies of thick annuli: under a torque, the buckling torque of a clamped annulus with \(b/a = 0.5\) and \(h/a = 0.1\) is 53 % of the classical one [bagheri2019Torque], Table 2.

Laminate offset#

The laminate spans \(d - h/2 \le z \le d + h/2\) about the reference plane, \(d\) being Shell.offset, as for the other models of the CLPT and of the FSDT. The reference plane being flat, \(z\) enters only the integrals through the thickness: the stiffness through the matrix ABD of the laminate about the reference plane, the coupling \(B\) included, and the mass through the moments \(I_0, I_1, I_2\) of the density, with the displacement field \(u(z)\), \(v(z)\), \(w(z)\) above. The connections tie the reference planes of the connected panels.

Numerical integration#

The coefficients \(1/\rho\) make the integrands rational functions of \(x\). The integrals of Bardell’s functions, Eq. (1), do not apply, and every matrix, also of the whole domain with a constant laminate, is integrated with the Gauss-Legendre rule. The rule converges exponentially, the faster the farther the centre \(\rho = 0\) is from the sector, i.e. the smaller \(a/r\).

Connection to a cylinder#

The local axes of the annular sector are those of the flange of the connection 'BFxcte', see Multidomain connections: the axes of the cylinder rotated by 90 degrees about the common circumferential axis \(y\), with \(\boldsymbol{e}_x\) of the sector along \(-\boldsymbol{e}_z\) of the cylinder (inwards) and \(\boldsymbol{e}_z\) of the sector along the axis \(\boldsymbol{e}_x\) of the cylinder. The web of a ring frame whose outer radius is the radius of the cylinder and which spans the angle of a cylinder domain shares the natural coordinate \(\eta\) with the edge \(x = x_f\) of that domain, and

dict(p1=cylinder, p2=web, func='BFxcte', xcte1=x_f, xcte2=0)

imposes, at the frame station \(x_f\), \(u_{cyl} = w_{web}\), \(v_{cyl} = v_{web}\), \(w_{cyl} = -u_{web}\) and the continuity of the rotation about the circumferential axis, with penalties or with the null space of MultiDomain. A closed ring is made of several sectors connected with 'SSycte' along their radial edges, as the domains of the cylinder.

The web of a ring frame is attached to a surface of the skin. With the key 'face' of 'BFxcte' the edge of the sector is connected to the inner surface of the cylinder, \(z = d - h/2\), or to its outer surface, \(z = d + h/2\), \(d\) being the offset and \(h\) the thickness of the cylinder, see Multidomain connections; 'lower' and 'upper' refer to the coordinate \(z\) of the cylinder, which points outwards, not to that of the sector. An internal frame, its outer edge on the inner surface, has the outer radius \(r - h/2 + d\):

dict(p1=cylinder, p2=web, func='BFxcte', xcte1=x_f, xcte2=0,
     face='lower')

and an external frame, its inner edge \(x = a\) on the outer surface, the inner radius \(r + h/2 + d\):

dict(p1=cylinder, p2=web, func='BFxcte', xcte1=x_f, xcte2=web.a,
     face='upper')

the points of the surface following the normal of the cylinder, \(u(z) = u - z w_{,x}\), \(v(z) = (1 + z/r) v - z w_{,y}\), with the Sanders-Koiter kinematics whatever the model of the cylinder, so that a rigid rotation about the axis is free of strain also with a Donnell cylinder. The sector has no eccentricity of its own: the radius of the connected edge must be that of the connected surface, and the sector must span the angle of the cylinder domain, \(b_{web}/r_{web} = b/r\), or the connection raises a ValueError. The cylinder with its reference surface at its inner face, offset = h/2, connected without the key, is the same internal frame.

The sectors of the first-order shear deformation theory connect in the same way to the cylinders with five degrees of freedom per term, e.g. 'cylshell_fsdt_sanders', with or without the key 'face'; the rotation about the circumferential axis tied by the connection is then \(\phi_x\) of both panels. The two panels of a connection must have the same number of degrees of freedom per term: an 'annular_fsdt_*' sector on a cylinder of the classical theory, or the converse, raises a NotImplementedError.

Third-order shear deformation theory#

The models 'annular_tsdt_donnell' and 'annular_tsdt_gl' are the annular sectors above with the third-order shear deformation theory (TSDT) of Reddy [reddy1984HigherOrder], the polar counterparts of 'plate_tsdt_donnell' and 'plate_tsdt_gl', with the five degrees of freedom \(u, v, w, \phi_x, \phi_y\) and the geometry, the coordinates and the local basis of the classical models. The displacement field is that of the flat plate, Third-order shear deformation theory with a laminate offset, with the derivative of \(w\) along the arc, \(s w_{,y}\), in place of \(w_{,y}\):

\[u(z) = u + z \phi_x - c_1 \left[(z - d)^3 + d^3\right] \left(\phi_x + w_{,x}\right) \qquad v(z) = v + z \phi_y - c_1 \left[(z - d)^3 + d^3\right] \left(\phi_y + s w_{,y}\right)\]

with \(w(z) = w\), \(c_1 = 4/(3 h^2)\) and the offset \(d\), such that the transverse shear strains

\[\gamma_{xz} = \left(\phi_x + w_{,x}\right) \left(1 - 3 c_1 (z - d)^2\right) \qquad \gamma_{yz} = \left(\phi_y + s w_{,y}\right) \left(1 - 3 c_1 (z - d)^2\right)\]

vanish at both faces of the laminate. The reference plane being flat, the metric does not depend on \(z\), and the in-plane strains at \(z\) are the linear membrane strains in polar coordinates,

\[\mathcal{L}(f, g) = \left\{ f_{,x},\ s g_{,y} - \frac{f}{\rho},\ s f_{,y} + g_{,x} + \frac{g}{\rho} \right\}^\top\]

of the in-plane displacements \(u(z)\), \(v(z)\), plus the non-linear membrane terms at the reference plane:

\[\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}(z) = \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^{(0)} + z \mathcal{L}(\phi_x, \phi_y) - c_1 \left[(z - d)^3 + d^3\right] \mathcal{L}\left(\phi_x + w_{,x},\ \phi_y + s w_{,y}\right)\]

with \(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}^{(0)}\) the membrane strains of 'annular_clpt_donnell' for 'annular_tsdt_donnell' and of 'annular_clpt_gl' for 'annular_tsdt_gl'. Since \(\partial s/\partial x = s/\rho\), the operator applied to \(s w_{,y}\) gives the terms \(s w_{,xy} + s w_{,y}/\rho\) and \(s^2 w_{,yy}\). With \(\phi_x = -w_{,x}\) and \(\phi_y = -s w_{,y}\) the transverse shear strains vanish, the third-order terms too, and the changes of curvature \(\mathcal{L}(\phi_x, \phi_y)\) are those of the classical models above; for \(r \to \infty\) the strains are those of 'plate_tsdt_donnell' and 'plate_tsdt_gl'.

The thirteen generalized strains are in the order of the constitutive matrix of the TSDT, Shell.ABD, which contains the offset as for the flat plate, see Strains, stiffness and mass matrices; the mass matrix integrates the kinetic energy of the displacement field above through the thickness, with the moments of the density about the mid-surface of the laminate. As for the classical models, all the matrices are integrated with the Gauss-Legendre rule. The rotations \(\phi_x\) and \(\phi_y\) are those of the normal at the mid-surface of the laminate; the sectors of a ring are joined with 'SSycte' along their radial edges.

Total-Lagrangian third-order theory#

The model 'annular_tsdt_tl' writes the field of the TSDT for finite rotations, as 'annular_fsdt_tl' does for the first-order theory: the deformed position of a point is

\[\boldsymbol{r}(z) = \boldsymbol{X} + z \boldsymbol{d} + p(z) \boldsymbol{q}, \qquad p(z) = (z - d)^3 + d^3, \qquad \boldsymbol{q} = -c_1 \left(\boldsymbol{d} - \boldsymbol{N}\right)\]

with \(\boldsymbol{X}\) the position of the deformed reference plane, the second-order director \(\boldsymbol{d}\) of 'annular_fsdt_tl' and the normal of the deformed reference plane, not normalized, \(\boldsymbol{N} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \times \boldsymbol{g}_2\). The linear part of \(\boldsymbol{d} - \boldsymbol{N}\) is the vector \((\phi_x + w_{,x}, \phi_y + s w_{,y})\) of the third-order term above. In the local basis, which turns with \(\theta\), a vector \(\boldsymbol{V}\) has \(\boldsymbol{V}_{,x} = \{V_{1,x}, V_{2,x}, V_{3,x}\}^\top\) and \(s \boldsymbol{V}_{,y} = \{s V_{1,y} + V_2/\rho,\ s V_{2,y} - V_1/\rho,\ s V_{3,y}\}^\top\). The strains are the coefficients of \(1\), \(z\) and \(p(z)\) of the Green-Lagrange strains of \(\boldsymbol{r}(z)\), and of \((z - d)^2\) in the transverse shear strains: the membrane strains, \(\boldsymbol{\kappa}\) and \(\boldsymbol{\gamma}\) of 'annular_fsdt_tl', and

\[ \begin{align}\begin{aligned}\kappa^{(3)}_{xx} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{q}_{,x} \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{yy} = \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot s \boldsymbol{q}_{,y} \qquad \kappa^{(3)}_{xy} = \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot s \boldsymbol{q}_{,y} + \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{q}_{,x}\\\gamma^{(2)}_{yz} = 3 \boldsymbol{g}_2 \cdot \boldsymbol{q} = -3 c_1 \gamma_{yz} \qquad \gamma^{(2)}_{xz} = 3 \boldsymbol{g}_1 \cdot \boldsymbol{q} = -3 c_1 \gamma_{xz}\end{aligned}\end{align} \]

the last two because \(\boldsymbol{g}_\alpha \cdot \boldsymbol{N} = 0\). Since also \(\boldsymbol{g}_\alpha \cdot \boldsymbol{N}_{,\beta} = -\boldsymbol{g}_{\alpha,\beta} \cdot \boldsymbol{N}\), the third-order strains are \(-c_1\) times the changes of curvature of the director plus the second fundamental form of the deformed plane, in place of \(\phi_{\alpha, \beta}\) and \(w_{,\alpha\beta}\) of the linear theory; they need the second derivatives of \(u\) and \(v\) as well. As in the linear TSDT, the terms in \(z^2\), \(z p\) and \(p^2\) of the in-plane strains, the other powers of the transverse shear strains and the transverse normal strain are dropped.

The transverse shear strains \(\boldsymbol{\gamma} (1 - 4 (z - d)^2/h^2)\) vanish at both faces for any deformation, Reddy’s condition. Writing the director part with the second-order director and keeping the third-order strains of the linear theory instead would not keep it: the face shear would be the difference between the shear of the director, \(\boldsymbol{g}_\alpha \cdot \boldsymbol{d}\), and the linear one, \(\phi_\alpha + w_{,\alpha}\), which cannot both vanish in large bending, and the transverse shear energy would stiffen the bending; for a thin flat strip, \(L/h = 500\), with a tip rotation of 0.46 rad, that choice gives a tip deflection 25 % below the elastica. With the construction above the linear part of the strains is that of 'annular_tsdt_donnell', the membrane strains are those of 'annular_tsdt_gl', a finite rigid rotation by the angle \(t\) changes the strains by \(O(t^4)\) or less, through the length of the second-order director, and for \(r \to \infty\) the strains are those of 'plate_tsdt_tl'. The tangent stiffness contains all the stress resultants, \(\boldsymbol{H}(\boldsymbol{\sigma}, \boldsymbol{d})\) being linear in the generalized derivatives; the mass matrix is that of the linear field above.
